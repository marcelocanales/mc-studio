Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

MC Studio System ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels it manages. It is used only by that owner.

What the app accesses

Through YouTube API Services, the app uploads and edits videos on the owner's own channels and reads those channels' statistics and analytics (views, watch time, likes, subscribers).

What the app stores

Authorization tokens and the channels' video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, store or process data about any other person.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Contact

Through the Gigantica channel page.
